2. What data does the app process?

2.1 Apple Calendar (Full Access)

The app reads calendar events from Apple Calendar to display the weekly view. Events are processed exclusively on your device and are never transmitted to external servers. Write access only occurs when you explicitly create or edit events.

2.2 Apple Reminders (Full Access)

The app reads and writes reminders to load and mark children's routines as completed. This processing also takes place exclusively locally on your device.

2.3 Local App Settings

Selected reminder lists, selected calendars and other app preferences are stored in UserDefaults locally. This data does not leave your device (except through iCloud Backup, if enabled by you).

2.4 No data collection by the developer

No usage data, crash reports, analytics or any other information is transmitted to developer-owned servers. There is no proprietary server infrastructure.

3. Legal basis for processing

The processing of calendar and reminder data is based on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent). You give your consent by granting the required system permissions to the app on first launch. You can revoke these permissions at any time in iOS/macOS Settings.

4. iCloud – Data with Apple

If iCloud is enabled on your device for Calendar and Reminders, Apple syncs your data according to their own privacy policy. In this case, Apple Inc. (One Apple Park Way, Cupertino, CA 95014, USA) is the data controller for iCloud synchronisation. Apple relies on Standard Contractual Clauses pursuant to Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR for transfers to the USA.

More information: apple.com/privacy
